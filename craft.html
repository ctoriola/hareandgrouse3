Research method

How we study
a single object

Step 01

Provenance

Tracing where a piece was made, by whom, and every hand it has passed through.

Step 02

Material & Technique

Examining fibre, clay, pigment and tool marks to understand how it was made.

Step 03

Cultural Context

Working with communities and scholars to understand meaning, use and ceremony.

Step 04

Care & Conservation

Recording condition and advising on how the work should be kept for the next generation.

Step 05

Sharing

Publishing research notes and building it into exhibitions and education programmes.
