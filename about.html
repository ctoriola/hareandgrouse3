Our name

Why the hare
and the grouse?

Two creatures of field and moor, painted, woven and carved by makers for centuries. They stand for what we care about: art rooted in place, patience and close observation — and for the pairing of the old with the new that runs through every exhibition we make.

Our intertwined monogram carries the same idea: two letters, two traditions, one mark.
