Who we are

Keeping the handmade
in circulation

Our collection reflects a high value placed on craft. From hand-woven cloth and wood-fired vessels to painting and carved forms, we bring artists, heritage makers and collectors together around works worth keeping — and tell the stories behind them.

What we do

Three pillars,
one purpose

Everything we present rests on careful study, thoughtful display and open access to culture.

01

Research

We study the histories, makers and materials behind every piece — provenance, technique and the communities that shaped it.

02

Exhibitions

Curated shows that place contemporary painting beside heritage craft, so old and new speak to one another.

03

Cultural Education

Talks, workshops, school programmes and writing that open the collection to everyone, not just collectors.
