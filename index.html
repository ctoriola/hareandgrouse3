About Hare & Grouse

Keeping the handmade, the historic & the cultural in circulation.

Hare & Grouse exhibits and promotes historical and cultural arts and crafts. Our collection reflects a high value placed on craft, and our work continues through research, exhibitions and cultural education — bringing artists and collectors together around works worth keeping.

Research

We study the histories, makers and materials behind every piece we present.

Exhibitions

Curated shows that place contemporary painting beside heritage craft.

Cultural Education

Talks, programmes and writing that open the collection to everyone.
